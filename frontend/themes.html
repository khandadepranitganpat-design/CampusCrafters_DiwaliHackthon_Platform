How Track Selection Works

1

Preferred Track

During registration, select your preferred track. This helps organizers assign appropriate mentors to your team channels.

2

Cross-Skill Teams

Teammates can have different skill levels. A team with a first-time coder and experienced backend dev can compete in any track.

3

Final Confirmation

Official track selection is locked when you submit your project repository and demo before the 24-hour sprint deadline.
