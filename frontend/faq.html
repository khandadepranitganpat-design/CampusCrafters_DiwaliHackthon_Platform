Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about Campus Sprint? Find detailed answers on eligibility, online team rules, tracks, submission formats, and judging criteria below.

🔍
Campus Sprint is a 24-hour online hackathon created by CampusCrafters to foster student innovation. Teams build working technical projects solving practical challenges across four innovation tracks: FinTech, Social Good, Web3, and Beginner.
CampusCrafters is a student developer platform empowering college builders to create, connect, and lead. Your CampusCrafters account allows you to participate in hackathons, preserve your project portfolio, and connect with teammates and mentors year-round.
Participation for this edition is open to currently enrolled students of [COLLEGE]. Students from all academic years (1st through 4th year and postgraduate) and all majors are eligible.
No! Campus Sprint is 100% free for all registered and verified students. You will never be asked for entry fees, ticket costs, or payment information.
Sign in or create a simulated CampusCrafters account, then complete the 5-step registration stepper. You will confirm your student details, choose primary skills, pick a preferred track, and accept the online hackathon rules. On completion, you receive an official registration badge and ticket pass.
Every hackathon submission must come from a team of 2 to 5 members. Solo submissions are not permitted to encourage team collaboration.
Yes! Individual participants can complete registration first. We have dedicated team-formation channels on our Discord where you can pitch ideas, list your skills, and form or join a team with a 6-character team join code before kickoff.
Yes! Multidisciplinary and mixed-year teams are actively encouraged. A first-year designer, a second-year frontend dev, and a fourth-year backend developer make a great combination.
The four tracks are: FinTech (payments, budgeting, financial tools), Social Good (education, healthcare, environment, civic tech), Web3 (decentralized apps, smart contracts, open web), and Beginner (first-time builders, mentored guidance). Visit the Themes page for full details.
You can explore demonstration challenges right now on the Problem Statements Directory. Note that official competition problem statements unlock simultaneously for all teams at kickoff on Nov 15 at 10:00 AM IST.
Yes. The track selected during individual registration is a preferred track to help with mentor allocation. Your team's official track is confirmed when you submit your final project repository before the 24-hour sprint deadline.
Campus Sprint 2026 is a 100% online hackathon. There is no physical venue attendance required. You and your teammates can collaborate from anywhere with your laptop, internet connection, and Discord.
You will need:
  • A laptop with code editor of your choice
  • A GitHub account to host your project repository
  • A Discord account to join mentor channels, announcements, and office hours
  • Your CampusCrafters account for dashboard and submission portal access
No pre-built applications are allowed. All core project code must be written during the 24-hour sprint window. However, you are freely encouraged to use open-source libraries, UI frameworks, public APIs, starter templates (e.g. Create React App, Vite, Next.js), and cloud services.
A complete project submission must include:
  • A public GitHub repository link with a clear README explaining setup and architecture
  • A working deployed live demo link or PWA link
  • A short recorded video walkthrough demo (max 3 minutes) demonstrating functionality
  • Selected problem statement and chosen track
Projects are evaluated on four key criteria:
  • Innovation & Creativity: Originality of the approach and problem framing
  • Technical Execution: Code quality, working demo, and architectural soundess
  • Practical Impact: Potential real-world benefit to users or community
  • Presentation: Clarity of video demo, documentation, and user experience
The Grand Prize is a $5,000 Team Cash Prize plus fast-track entry into the CampusCrafters regional finals. Track winners receive $1,500 Cash each for FinTech and Social Good, and custom mechanical keyboards plus lifetime Pro accounts for the Best Beginner team.
During event day, our online help desk runs continuously on Discord. You can open a ticket in the #help-desk channel, drop into mentor voice rooms, or email us at support@campuscrafters.com.
