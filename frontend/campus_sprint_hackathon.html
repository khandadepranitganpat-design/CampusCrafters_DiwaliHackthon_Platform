How it works

1

Join & Register

Log in with your CampusCrafters account and answer a few event questions to secure your spot.

2

Form a team

Create a team or join one with a code. Teams must have exactly 2 to 5 members.

3

Build (24h)

Problem statements unlock at kickoff. Grab your laptop, grab some coffee, and start building.

4

Demo & Win

Submit your project repo online and present your demo on site to the judges.

The 24 Hours

Everything you need to know about event day.

9A

Virtual Lobby Opens

Virtual lobby and Discord go live. Join the server and complete your digital check-in.

10A

Kickoff Ceremony

Opening ceremony and problem statements unlock. Hacking begins!

1P

Mentor Check-In

Mentors join the Discord. Book a 1-on-1 slot or drop into the open help channel.

7P

Late-Night Support

Mentor availability continues. Stretch break activities and community channels stay active.

12A

Midnight Check-In

Optional async check-in on Discord. Organizers online for questions and support.

10A

Submissions Close

Hacking officially ends. All repos and decks must be submitted.

1:30P

Closing Ceremony

Project expo finishes, winners announced, and prizes distributed.
